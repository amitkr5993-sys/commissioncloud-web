Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Commission Cloud (available at https://commissioncloud.in). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users.

1. Information We Collect

To provide tracking and cashback services, we collect minimal personal information, which may include:

  • Name and Email Address (during registration or login).
  • Browser session cookies, IP addresses, and device details for security and fraud prevention.
  • Order identification numbers and click tracking tokens generated when redirecting to partner store links.

2. Affiliate Links and Tracking Cookies

Commission Cloud participates in affiliate marketing programs provided by networks such as INRDeals and Cuelinks. When you click on merchant offers (e.g., Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra) through our platform:

  • A secure cookie or tracking parameter is stored on your device by the affiliate partner.
  • This tracking allows the merchant to verify the sale and credit the applicable cashback/commission to Commission Cloud.
  • We do not collect or store your payment card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords used during merchant checkouts.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Tracking referred transactions and crediting cashback rewards to your wallet.
  • Preventing fraudulent clicks, duplicate conversions, and abuse of our platform.
  • Responding to user support queries and account verification requests.

4. Data Security

We implement secure HTTPS encryption (SSL/TLS) across our web domains to safeguard all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.

5. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains outgoing links to external e-commerce sites. We have no direct control over the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of the destination stores.
