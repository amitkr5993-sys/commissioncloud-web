Referral Logic & Terms:

1. ₹50 Commission Condition: The ₹25 referral bonus applies only when a referred friend's qualifying order generates at least ₹50 in total commission.

2. Orders Below ₹50 Commission: If a friend's first order generates less than ₹50 in total commission (for example, ₹30), no ₹25 referral bonus is triggered for that order. Your friend still receives their standard commission share on that order.

3. Qualifying Later Order: If the referred friend later places another order (for example, generating ₹50 or ₹60 in total commission) and meets the ₹50 minimum commission requirement for the first time, the one-time ₹25 referral bonus is unlocked on that qualifying order.

4. One-Time Bonus per Friend: Each referred friend can unlock the ₹25 referral bonus only once on their first qualifying ₹50+ commission order.

5. Order Cancellation Rule: If an order is cancelled, rejected, or refunded, all associated payouts—including user share, referral bonus, and admin share—are reversed to ₹0.
